Checking...

Session-only keys are cleared when Chrome restarts. Remembered keys are stored in Chrome extension storage on this device, which is not an encrypted password vault.

What Gemini receives

When Gemini is selected, Review Author sends reviewer notes, the scraped product title and description, and revision feedback needed for the request.

It does not send Amazon cookies, account details, purchase history, full page HTML, or unrelated page content.
